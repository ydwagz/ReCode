How a day works

The whole loop fits in 24 hours, and it starts over every night.

  1. 00:00

    The problem drops

    A new challenge opens for everyone at midnight UTC.

  2. Start

    Your clock starts

    Speed is measured from when you press start, not from midnight.

  3. Live

    The board moves

    Every accepted solution lands on the leaderboard as it happens.

  4. 24:00

    Solutions unlock

    The explanation and everyone's shared code open up for discussion.

What earns points

60CorrectnessEvery test must pass. There's no partial credit.
20AttemptsEach failed submission costs a little more than the one before.
10SpeedFull points if you solve it within five minutes of starting.
10EfficiencyRuntime and memory, compared with the reference solution.

Ties go to whoever solved it faster.
